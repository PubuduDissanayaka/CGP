Pre-Departure Checklist

What to arrange, pack and understand before you fly to Germany - a practical checklist for CGP students preparing to leave Sri Lanka.

May be distributed freely · Not an offer of placement or visa

Use with CGP orientation: this checklist supports CGP pre-departure orientation, which covers your specific case, your institution and current requirements. Use it to prepare, and bring your questions to orientation.

01Documents to carry

  • Passport & visa

    Your passport with the German visa, plus a few photocopies kept separately from the originals.

  • Training contract / confirmation

    The training contract or letter of confirmation from your German healthcare institution.

  • Original certificates & translations

    The originals and certified translations your institution or registration needs.

  • Medical certificates

    Any medical documents required for registration or your employer.

  • Photographs

    Passport-size photographs for registration and paperwork in Germany.

  • Proof of financial means

    The documents you needed to show for your visa, kept safe.

02Financial arrangements

  • Understand your training allowance

    How and when your monthly training allowance is paid, and banking details arranged for Germany.

  • First weeks in Germany covered

    Make sure you have the funds to cover your first weeks before the first allowance payment arrives.

  • Inform your Sri Lankan bank

    Notify your bank that you are travelling, so cards are not blocked abroad.

03Accommodation & arrival

  • Confirm accommodation

    Know exactly where you are staying on arrival. Accommodation arrangements vary between institutions - confirm the specifics before you travel.

  • Arrival pickup and timing

    Confirm how you will reach your accommodation from the airport, and who to contact if your flight is delayed.

  • First contact in Germany

    Have the phone number or contact details of your institution and of CGP support for after you land.

04Packing essentials

  • Medications

    Any personal medication, with a doctor's letter if it is a controlled medicine. Confirm what you may bring into Germany before packing.

  • Season-appropriate clothing

    Clothing for the German season you are arriving in, including warm layers for winter and waterproofs for wet weather.

  • Comfort / work basics

    Comfortable, hard-wearing shoes and a small supply of personal items that are expensive or hard to find soon after arrival.

  • Phone & chargers

    Unlocked phone with chargers and adapters. Consider how you will get a German SIM on arrival.

05Practical understanding before you fly

  • Workplace expectations

    Understand how German healthcare workplaces operate: punctuality, direct communication and professional boundaries.

  • Daily life basics

    How public transport, supermarkets, appointments and paperwork work in Germany.

  • Registration expectations

    Know which registrations and appointments happen in your first weeks, so you are not caught by surprise.

  • Your first days at the institution

    Where to report on your first day, who your contact is, and what to bring.

06Stay connected with CGP

  • Confirm your contact details with CGP before you leave.
  • Keep CGP's contact details with you in Germany.
  • Arrival support and integration support continue after you land - CGP stays in touch as you settle into training.
