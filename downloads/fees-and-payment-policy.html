Fees & Payment Policy

The written fee structure and payment schedule for the CGP programme, including the no-hidden-charges commitment.

May be distributed freely · Not an offer of placement or visa

The commitment in one sentence: you receive the exact fee amounts and the payment schedule in writing before you commit to anything. Nothing is added later.

01The three fees

“How much will this cost me, and are there any hidden charges?” is the first question almost every family asks. Here is the structure in full.

Fee What it covers
Registration Enrolment as a CGP student, assessment and counselling.
German language course Structured German training towards the required level.
CGP service fee Applications, documents, interview coordination, visa guidance, pre-departure and arrival support.

02Payment schedule

The payment schedule is agreed in writing before you commit to the pathway. Exact amounts and the schedule are confirmed directly with you on enquiry - and are published on the CGP website as they are finalised.

03No hidden charges

  • The three fees above are the complete set. There are no hidden charges at any stage.
  • If a genuine cost arises for your specific case - for example an official fee charged by an authority, or a recognised translation - it will be explained and confirmed with you in advance, never added silently.
  • Nothing is charged that you were not told about in writing first.

04Before you pay anything

  1. Ask for the written fee structure and payment schedule.
  2. Confirm exactly which services your payment covers.
  3. Keep the written confirmation - it is the record of your agreement.
  4. Ask any question, however basic. CGP would rather you asked.

Ethical recruitment: CGP is built on transparency, ethical recruitment and student welfare first. If anything about fees is unclear at any stage, raising it is always welcome and always free.

05Questions

For any question about fees or payments, message CGP on WhatsApp or email. All amounts and schedules are confirmed in writing before any commitment is made.

Contact: hello@cgp.lk · WhatsApp via the CGP website (cgp.lk) · Monday–Friday, 9:00–17:00 Sri Lanka time.
