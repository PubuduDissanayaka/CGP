Document Checklist

Every certificate, translation and supporting document you will need for the CGP Nursing Ausbildung pathway - grouped in the order you will need it.

May be distributed freely · Not an offer of placement or visa

Important: exact requirements depend on the German institution you are matched with, the current German rules that apply to your application, and your individual circumstances. CGP confirms the precise list for your case during assessment and counselling. This checklist is the general structure - use it to start gathering documents, but do not treat it as the final list for a particular application.

01Identity

  • National Identity Card (NIC)

    Copy of both sides.

  • Passport

    Valid passport; copies of the photo page and all pages with stamps or entries. Check the expiry date early - renewing late can delay the process.

  • Passport-size photographs

    Recent photographs on a plain background, meeting the specifications your application requires.

02Education

  • School leaving certificates

    G.C.E. Ordinary Level and Advanced Level certificates, or equivalent qualifications.

  • Higher education certificates & transcripts

    Any diplomas, degrees, or completed coursework, with transcripts where available.

  • Professional or vocational certificates

    Any nursing, healthcare or care-related training or qualifications already completed.

  • English / other language certificates

    Any recognised language certificates you hold.

03German language

  • German course enrolment or completion records

    Certificates or records from CGP German language training, showing the level you have reached.

  • Recognised language test results

    Results from the recognised German test your application requires (for example Goethe or telc at the required CEFR level, A1–B2), issued by the testing body.

04Work experience

  • Work certificates & reference letters

    For any employment, internships or volunteer experience, especially in healthcare or care settings.

  • Curriculum vitae (CV)

    A clear, current CV covering education, experience and skills. CGP helps you prepare this.

05Health & character

  • Medical fitness certificate

    A medical certificate confirming you are fit to work in healthcare, issued by a recognised medical practitioner when required by the process.

  • Police clearance certificate

    The certificate of good conduct that your application requires, obtained from the relevant Sri Lankan authority.

06Translations & legalisation

  • Certified translations

    Translated versions of certificates and documents, completed by a recognised translator where the process requires them.

  • Attestation / apostille

    Official attestation of documents where the German institution or authority requires it.

07Financial & residence

  • Proof of financial means

    Evidence required to show you can support yourself, as specified by the visa process for your case.

  • Visa application forms & supporting evidence

    Completed visa forms plus all supporting evidence required for the German visa process, prepared with CGP guidance.

  • Training contract / confirmation from the institution

    The training contract or letter of confirmation from the German healthcare institution once your position is confirmed.

08Good practice

Whenever you gather documents for CGP or for an application:

  • Supply clear copies of originals - never send originals by email.
  • Keep the originals safe; you will need them for attestation and at later stages.
  • Tell CGP about any name differences across documents early - they can cause delays later.
  • Check expiry dates (passport, medical certificates) before they become a problem.

Privacy: your personal information and documents are handled carefully and kept secure throughout the process. Never email personal documents before CGP tells you that a specific document is needed.
