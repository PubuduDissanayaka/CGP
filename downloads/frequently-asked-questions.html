Frequently Asked Questions

The ten questions CGP is asked most about Nursing Ausbildung in Germany - with straight answers, in one printable document.

May be distributed freely · Not an offer of placement or visa

01

What is Nursing Ausbildung in Germany?

Ausbildung is Germany's dual vocational training system. For nursing, it combines classroom study with paid, hands-on training inside a real healthcare institution, and leads to a state-recognised German nursing qualification.

02

Do I need to know German before applying?

You do not need German to make your first enquiry - but you do need it to succeed. German language training is a core part of the CGP pathway, and reaching the level required by employers and authorities is a genuine requirement of the process, not an optional extra.

03

What are the eligibility requirements?

The pathway suits motivated students, typically around 18 to 25, who want to build a professional healthcare career in Germany and are prepared to commit to language training. Exact requirements depend on the German institution and the current rules that apply to your application, so CGP confirms them with you during assessment and counselling rather than publishing a generic list.

04

How much does the CGP programme cost?

There are three fee components: registration, the German language course, and the CGP service fee. The structure is set out on the CGP website and in the Fees & Payment Policy document, and the exact amounts and payment schedule are confirmed in writing before you commit. There are no hidden charges.

05

What does CGP support me with during the application and visa process?

Application preparation, document preparation and checking, employer interview coordination and preparation, and step-by-step visa guidance. If something goes wrong or a document is queried, you have a named point of contact rather than being left to work it out alone.

06

How long does the process take?

It depends mainly on two things: how quickly you progress through German language training, and the timelines of the German institution you are matched with. CGP gives you a realistic timeline during counselling and updates it honestly as you progress - CGP would rather tell you it will take longer than promise a date it cannot control.

07

Will CGP support me after I arrive in Germany?

Yes. Arrival support and integration support are part of the pathway, and ongoing student support continues while you are settling into your training.

08

Is accommodation available in Germany?

Accommodation arrangements vary between German healthcare institutions. It is one of the specifics CGP confirms with the institution for your particular placement, and it is covered fully in pre-departure orientation so you know exactly what applies before you travel.

09

Can I work after completing Nursing Ausbildung?

A completed Nursing Ausbildung is a state-recognised German qualification, and it opens routes to continued employment, specialisation and further study within German healthcare. CGP will not promise you a specific job or salary - what CGP can do is prepare you properly for the qualification.

10

How do I apply to become a CGP student?

Message CGP on WhatsApp or send an enquiry through the contact page. CGP will talk you through the pathway, the requirements and the fees, and assess whether Nursing Ausbildung is the right route for you before anyone commits to anything.
