01

Who we are

Wilimbula · Gampaha District

We don't just recruit students. We prepare them - and stay with them.

Ceylon German Pathway (CGP) is based in Wilimbula, Henegama, in the Gampaha District, and serves Sri Lankan students and families nationwide. We help motivated young people pursue vocational education and healthcare career opportunities in Germany.

Most people who come to us are asking the same question: “I want to build a healthcare career in Germany, but I don't know how to start, what documents I need, how to learn German, or how to handle the Ausbildung and visa process.” Our entire service is built to answer that question properly, in order, with someone accountable at each step.

That means candidate selection, German language training, application and document preparation, employer interview coordination, visa guidance, pre-departure orientation, and support once you have arrived in Germany.

What we believe

Five working principles

Principles, not slogans.

2.1

Ethical recruitment

Student welfare comes before placement numbers. We are honest about requirements, timelines and what the pathway does and does not involve.

2.2

Transparency

Our fees and payment policy are written down and explained before you commit. No hidden charges at any stage.

2.3

Student welfare

We treat every student as a person building a career, not a file to be processed.

2.4

Continuous support

Our responsibility runs from the first enquiry through to integration support after arrival in Germany.

2.5

Privacy

Your personal information and documents are handled carefully and kept secure throughout the process.

04

How we work

Illustrative examples

Two examples of the model in practice.

Please read: the two examples below describe how the CGP service model is designed to work. They are illustrative of our process - they are not accounts of specific completed cases, and no outcome is implied or promised. Real, verified case studies will replace them as they are confirmed.

Nursing Ausbildung candidate preparation

Situation
A motivated Sri Lankan student wants to pursue a Nursing Ausbildung in Germany but needs structured guidance on the application process, German language preparation, documentation, interviews and visa requirements.
What CGP does
Guides the student through the preparation pathway: German language training, professional preparation, document support, interview preparation and pre-departure guidance.
Intended result
The student progresses through the CGP preparation pathway with a clear understanding of the requirements and is better prepared for the next stages of the Germany journey.

German healthcare partnership

Situation
A German healthcare institution is looking for a reliable international partner to help identify and prepare suitable candidates from Sri Lanka.
What CGP does
Applies its partnership model: candidate selection, German language preparation, professional development, documentation, employer interviews, visa preparation and integration support.
Intended result
A structured pathway for institutions to work with motivated, professionally prepared Sri Lankan candidates, supporting long-term cooperation between Germany and Sri Lanka.
