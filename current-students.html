02

Support available to you

While you are on the pathway

You always have someone to ask.

2.01

Ongoing student support

A continuing point of contact for as long as you are on the pathway - for questions about your course, your documents or your timeline.

2.02

Document & visa help

If a document is queried or the visa process stalls, contact us. Getting stuck alone at this stage is exactly what CGP exists to prevent.

2.03

Interview preparation

Coaching and practice before every employer interview, plus a debrief afterwards.

2.04

Pre-departure orientation

Practical preparation for life in Germany - culture, workplace expectations and what happens when you land.

2.05

Arrival & integration support

Support in your first period in Germany and while you settle into your training and daily life.

2.06

Privacy of your documents

Your personal information and documents are handled carefully and kept secure throughout the process.
