07 German Partners Für Partnereinrichtungen

A partner, not a placement agency.

CGP works with German hospitals, clinics and care institutions as a long-term partner. We select motivated Sri Lankan candidates and prepare them properly - language, professional readiness and documentation - before they ever sit in front of your interview panel.

For
German hospitals, clinics and care institutions
Model
Long-term partnership, not one-off recruitment

01

Partnership services

What we provide

Prepared candidates. Reliable process.

1.01

Candidate Selection & Preparation

We identify motivated Sri Lankan candidates and prepare them - language, professional readiness and documentation - before they reach your interview panel.

1.02

Employer Interview Coordination

We coordinate and schedule interviews between your team and prepared candidates, and brief candidates thoroughly beforehand.

1.03

Partnership Development

We build long-term working relationships with German healthcare institutions rather than one-off placements.

02

How the partnership works

Seven stages

From introduction to integration.

The same structure applies to every partnership, so your team always knows which stage a candidate is at.

01

Introduction

We learn what your institution needs: roles, timing, language expectations and the profile of candidate that succeeds in your environment.

02

Candidate selection

We select from motivated Sri Lankan candidates already engaged with the CGP pathway, matched against your requirements.

03

Preparation

Language training and professional preparation continue against the level your institution requires.

04

Documentation

Certificates, translations and supporting paperwork are compiled and checked before they reach you.

05

Interviews

We coordinate interviews with your team and prepare candidates so the conversation is substantive on both sides.

06

Visa & departure

We guide the candidate through visa preparation and pre-departure orientation.

07

Arrival & integration

Arrival support and integration support continue after the candidate lands, so they settle into your institution properly.

03

The partnership model

Illustrative example

Please read: the example below describes how the CGP partnership model is designed to work. It is illustrative of our process - it is not an account of a specific completed partnership, and no outcome is implied or promised.

German healthcare partnership

Situation
A German healthcare institution is looking for a reliable international partner to help identify and prepare suitable candidates from Sri Lanka.
What CGP does
Candidate selection, German language preparation, professional development, documentation, employer interviews, visa preparation and integration support.
Intended result
A structured pathway for institutions to work with motivated, professionally prepared Sri Lankan candidates, while supporting long-term cooperation between Germany and Sri Lanka.
04

Why work with CGP

Four reasons

Preparation is the whole point.

Candidates arrive prepared

Language training, professional preparation and complete documentation happen before the interview, not after the contract.

One accountable partner

A single point of contact from selection through to integration, rather than a chain of intermediaries.

Ethical recruitment

Transparent fees and documented policies on the student side, so candidates arrive without hidden debts or false expectations.

Support after arrival

Integration support continues once the candidate joins you, which is when retention is actually decided.
