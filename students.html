Preparation · Education · Support

Your journey starts with preparation.

CGP supports young Sri Lankans through structured German-language education, professional and cultural preparation, and responsible guidance towards vocational training opportunities in Germany.

Best suited to Motivated students, around 18–25, aiming at healthcare in Germany.

Core requirement Commitment to systematic German-language learning.

Main offering Nursing Ausbildung Programme.

Programme Information & Transparency

Before you enrol

Clear and transparent communication, before you commit.

CGP believes in clear and transparent communication with students and their families. Detailed information regarding programme structure, German-language preparation, responsibilities, applicable educational and preparation costs, and payment arrangements is provided directly to prospective candidates before enrolment. No student should enrol without understanding the applicable programme conditions.
